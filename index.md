# Maximilian Wegener, MPH

<div class="contact">
Biomedical Informatics Librarian · Cushing/Whitney Medical Library · Yale School of Medicine
</div>

<div class="intro">

I'm an epidemiologist and biomedical informatics librarian at Yale. I help researchers find, clean, analyze, and share health data, and I teach hands-on workshops in R, the NIH *All of Us* Researcher Workbench, and AI-assisted programming.

Before joining the library, I spent over a decade in public health: infectious disease surveillance for the State of Nevada, HIV/HCV research with the Yale AIDS Program, and communicable disease investigation at the Washoe County Health District.

</div>
